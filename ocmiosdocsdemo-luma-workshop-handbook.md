## 2. Event Overview

Luma Lab Workshop is an online, hands-on learning series for business teams. The theme of this session is “Turning Repetitive Work into Reusable Methods.”

Who should attend:
- People who regularly prepare weekly reports, meeting notes, or business materials.
- Project leads who want to reduce repetitive preparation work.
- Beginners interested in improving their work practices. No programming experience is required.

Topics covered:
1. Identifying repetitive steps in everyday work.
2. Using a weekly report example to demonstrate how to develop a reusable method.
3. Completing a short exercise using a work scenario prepared by each attendee, and discussing how to check results and improve over time.

## 4. Preparation

Please prepare one repetitive work scenario you would like to improve, such as writing weekly reports, preparing meeting notes, or consolidating business materials.

Describe the following in two or three sentences:
- The outcome you want from the task.
- The steps you typically follow today.
- The step that takes the most time or is easiest to miss.

The live exercise will use this scenario. There is no need to upload real business files. Do not include customer information, personal information, or sensitive internal content in exercise materials; use fictional or sanitized examples instead.
